01 — About

One research thread, three vantage points

I'm a postdoctoral fellow at UNC Charlotte's Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, where I serve as project manager on an NSF Civic Innovation Challenge deployment of AI-enabled video analytics across ten pilot sites in Uptown Charlotte. My Ph.D. dissertation, Co-Creating Responsible AI for Public Safety, sits at the same intersection I've worked in for a decade: the place where policy design, statistical modeling, and lived human experience have to agree with each other before a system gets deployed.

That means my work doesn't split cleanly into "the policy paper" and "the ML paper." A single project — privacy-preserving video analytics for public safety — has produced federal grant writing, mixed-methods stakeholder research, supervised machine learning models, and UX evaluation frameworks, often in the same year. I teach graduate and undergraduate courses in data science and machine learning, mentor students across data analytics and UX research, and I'm still, underneath it, an industrial engineer who never stopped asking what a system does to the people inside it.

02 — Research

Selected publications

Spanning policy journals, HCI/UX venues, and computer vision conferences — three literatures, one research program. Full list on Google Scholar.

2026Policy

Co-Creating Responsible AI for Public Safety: A Mixed-Method Approach Toward Understanding Stakeholder Perceptions

Technological Forecasting and Social Change

A large-scale mixed-methods study of stakeholder perceptions of AI-assisted video surveillance, combining LLM-driven thematic analysis with a 410-respondent public survey. Introduces the Alignment–Tension–Gap framework and argues for a unified federal governance standard.

2026UX

Beyond the Interface: Redefining UX for Society-in-the-Loop AI Systems

Proc. International Conference on Sustainability, Innovation, and Society (ICSIS)

Proposes a sociotechnical UX framework for human-in-the-loop AI, moving evaluation beyond the interface toward a society-in-the-loop paradigm. Formalizes four new evaluative metrics spanning accuracy, latency, adaptation time, and trust calibration.

2026Data / ML

A Case Study in Responsible AI-Assisted Video Solutions: Multi-Metric Behavioral Insights in a Public Market Setting

Proc. International Conference on Human-AI Interaction and Experience Design (HAXD)

Extracts dwell time, directional flow, and movement-pattern insights from privacy-preserving, pose-based representations in a real-world public market deployment.

2026Data / ML

From Frames to Events: Rethinking Evaluation in Human-Centric Video Anomaly Detection

Proc. IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)

Proposes an event-centric evaluation framework for pose-based anomaly detection, exposing a critical gap between frame-level accuracy and real-world event-level precision in state-of-the-art models.

2025Data / ML

Towards Adaptive Human-Centric Video Anomaly Detection: A Comprehensive Framework and a New Benchmark

IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems for Video Technology

Introduces HuVAD, a privacy-centric anomaly detection benchmark built through original data collection, labeling, and annotation.

2025Policy

Demographic Disparities, Service Efficiency, Safety, and User Satisfaction in Public Bus Transit

Transportation Research Interdisciplinary Perspectives

A survey-based case study of Charlotte's public bus system, using quantitative methods to surface demographic gaps in service and inform data-driven transit policy.

2024Policy

Exploring Public's Perception of Safety and Video Surveillance Technology: A Survey Approach

Technology in Society

A public survey on the societal implications of video surveillance, capturing community concern and the case for equitable AI governance.

2023Policy

Understanding Policy and Technical Aspects of AI-Enabled Smart Video Surveillance to Address Public Safety

Computational Urban Science

A framework for balancing public safety, privacy, and ethics at the intersection of AI policy and deployment.

Additional publications in AAAI, IEEE IoT Journal, SMARTCOMP, and PLEA available on Google Scholar or in the full CV.
